## Ingredients

- 4 slices gluten-free bread (Schar Deli Style Bread)
- 16 oz deli turkey (Dietz & Watson Gourmet Lite Turkey Breast)
- 4 thin slices swiss cheese (Meijer Thin Sliced Swiss Cheese)
- 4 oz avocado, smashed (Wholly Diced Avocado)
- Russion-style dressing, see below
- 2 dill pickle spears

### Homemade Russian-style Dressing

- 2 Tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 Tbsp ketchup
- Hot sauce, such as Sriacha, to taste
- Dash of paprika or smoked paprika
- 1 dill pickle spear, chopped to relish

## Method

1. Warm the turkey in the oven.
2. Toast the bread.
3. Combine the dressing ingredients.
4. Smash the avocado. 
5. Build the sandwich, layering each level of turkey with the dressing.
6. Serve with pickle spear on the side.

## Nutrition

Makes two sandwiches. Each sandwich:

* 846 calories
* 62g protein
* 40g fat
* 58g carbohydrate
